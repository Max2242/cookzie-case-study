Track 02 · What we own · Shared ownership

A recipe commons for students away from home.

Grandmothers’ recipes, shared, credited and never overwritten. Students seed them, cook them and pass them along.

The brief

Design a product, a brand or a system for a world where everything you use belongs to everyone, and to no one alone.

Ownership as abundance, and ownership as neglect. Both are true.

The problem

Cooking away from home means missing the ingredients, the tools and the taste.

01

Some days there is food, some days there isn’t

Students have onions and rice one week and an empty shelf the next. They need to cook with whatever is in the room.

02

The taste of home is stuck with grandmothers

The best recipes live in her head, her diary and her voice. When she isn’t in the kitchen, they are hard to find.

03

Every room owns the same things alone

One cooker, one mixer, one spice rack in every room, mostly idle. Shared, a whole floor could cook far more.

The idea

Cookzie is a recipe commons.

A grandmother’s recipe belongs to every cook who has passed it along, and to no one alone.

01

Seed

A student adds their Nani’s recipe with a voice note, a photo and her name.

02

Cook

Other students cook it with what they have, using swaps and the shared floor shelf.

03

Change

After cooking, they add what they did differently. Only cooks can add changes.

04

Branch

The change joins the recipe’s family tree. The original is never overwritten.

Who owns what

The brief’s hard questions, answered in the product.

Who owns it?

The people who cook it

Cooking unlocks the right to review and add changes. Signing up isn’t enough.

Who maintains it?

Whoever cooked it last

After cooking, the app asks what you changed. Maintaining is a by-product of cooking.

What if someone takes more than their share?

Credit stays

Nani’s name stays on every version, so nobody can claim a recipe as their own.

What if someone wants out?

Families can leave

The family can withdraw a recipe at any time. Idle recipes are offered to a new keeper.
